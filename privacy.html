Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Leer en español

Summary

Kairox is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Information obtained from TikTok and other platforms stays on your device and is used only to publish the content you choose.

1. Information Kairox accesses from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit, and only with your permission, Kairox accesses:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): to send videos you select to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Direct post (video.publish): to publish videos you select, with the caption and privacy setting you choose.
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok (such as the privacy options available to your account), so you can choose valid options before posting.

2. How the information is used

  • To upload and publish videos you choose, at the time you choose.
  • To show you the connected account and the status of each post.

Kairox does not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose, and does not read your followers, messages or other users' content.

3. Where data is stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only in local files on your computer. Videos and captions are read from your computer and sent directly to the TikTok API (and to the other platforms you connect). No data obtained from TikTok is sent to Kairox or to any other third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The only transfer is the content you decide to publish, sent to the platform you choose.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account.
  • To delete them, choose Disconnect in Kairox → Accounts. This revokes the authorization with TikTok and removes the tokens from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Uninstalling Kairox and deleting its folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Other platforms

If you also connect YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, the same principles apply: access is granted through each platform's official authorization, tokens stay on your computer, and data is used only to publish your content.

7. Children

Kairox is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the platforms you connect).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: mauriigalleguillos@gmail.com

Política de privacidad (español)

Resumen

Kairox es una aplicación de escritorio que funciona en tu propio computador y no tiene servidores propios. No recopilamos, vendemos ni compartimos tus datos personales. La información obtenida de TikTok y otras plataformas se queda en tu equipo y solo se usa para publicar el contenido que tú eliges.

1. Información a la que accede Kairox

  • Perfil básico (user.info.basic): ID, nombre visible y foto, para mostrar qué cuenta está conectada.
  • Subida de videos (video.upload): para enviar a tu bandeja de TikTok los videos que eliges, como borradores.
  • Publicación directa (video.publish): para publicar los videos que eliges, con la descripción y privacidad que defines.
  • Ajustes de creador que entrega TikTok (por ejemplo, las opciones de privacidad disponibles), para que elijas opciones válidas antes de publicar.

2. Uso

Solo para subir y publicar los videos que eliges y mostrarte la cuenta conectada y el estado de cada publicación. No se usa para publicidad, perfiles, analítica ni ningún otro fin.

3. Almacenamiento

Los tokens de acceso se guardan solo en archivos locales de tu computador. Los videos se envían directamente a la API de TikTok (y a las otras plataformas que conectes). Ningún dato obtenido de TikTok se envía a Kairox ni a terceros.

4. Conservación y eliminación

  • Los tokens se guardan hasta que desconectas la cuenta en Kairox → Cuentas → Desconectar, lo que revoca la autorización en TikTok y los borra de tu equipo.
  • También puedes revocar el acceso desde la app de TikTok: Ajustes y privacidad → Seguridad → Administrar permisos de apps.
  • Al desinstalar Kairox y borrar su carpeta se eliminan todos los datos locales.
